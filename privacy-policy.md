## Overview

Topic Study (“the App”) is a topical Bible reading app. It is designed to work
offline, without user accounts.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect personal information.**

- The App does not require registration or login.
- Searches, topic views, and Bible reading happen on your device.
- We do not operate a server that receives your usage data.
- The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Local data stored on your device

The App stores a local copy of the Bible text and curated topic content on your
device so features work offline. This data does not leave your device through
the App.

## Third-party links (“Go Deeper”)

Some screens include links to partner sites or apps (for example study tools or
audio Bible services). If you tap those links:

- Your device opens the partner’s website or app (often in an in-app browser).
- Those third parties may collect information under their own privacy policies.
- If a link is an affiliate link, a commission may be paid to us when you make
  a qualifying purchase or sign up — at no additional cost to you.

We do not control third-party sites and are not responsible for their practices.

## Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s practices change. The “Last
updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be
reflected in the App Store / Google Play listing Privacy Policy URL.

## Bible text attribution

Scripture text in the App is from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a
public-domain (CC0) translation. See https://berean.bible for details.
